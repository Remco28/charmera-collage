the story behind

charmera collage

A free app that turns your photos into a collage.

Kodak’s Charmera is a tiny keychain camera that takes terrible pictures. Put a roll of them on one sheet, and they come to life.

Open charmera collage

An actual working camera

When I first saw the camera, it was so cute that I just had to pick it up and play with it. At first I thought it was just a toy keychain, but my friend told me it was an actual working camera. I had to go buy one.

I think that’s the same story for most owners of this camera. But everyone quickly realizes how terrible the pictures are. I’m sure there are millions of these cameras sitting in drawers somewhere.

On their own, the quality is quite terrible.
But when you put them in a collage, they come to life.

One minute, no ads

There are a lot of apps that make collages, but they’re all kind of terrible. And they make you pay for them. If they’re free, they usually come with a ton of ads, which makes me never want to open them. I wanted something where I can drag a whole bunch of photos from my computer and turn them into a collage, all within one minute. I couldn’t find anything, so I just built it myself.

How the minute goes

  1. 1. Drop in a roll

    Up to 12 photos, like a roll of film. Drag them anywhere on the page.

  2. 2. Pick a look

    Every look is drawn with your own photos, so you see it before you choose.

  3. 3. Snap it

    One button saves the sheet to your device as a PNG or JPG.

What it does

Every shot, full size

Nothing is cropped and nothing is shrunk. Every photo keeps its full picture, edge to edge, and the sheet grows to fit.

14 looks, each a real photo object

Contact sheets, one-hour prints, instant photos, a clothesline, a scrapbook, a stamp album, a slide lightbox and more, in 31 colorways.

Captions in the look’s own hand

Write on the back of any photo. It shows up in marker, ballpoint, typewriter or a gallery label, depending on the look. Drag photos to reorder them; on a phone, press and hold first.

Mix your own

Pick the exact background, border and caption colors. The first choices come from your own photos. Then choose the border width, and straight or tossed prints.

Big sheets, phone sizes too

Each file type shows its size before you save. iPhones can’t save very large images, so big sheets also offer a phone size.

No fuss

No account, no ads, no uploads: your photos never leave your browser. Pick a body color to match your camera, and the whole app follows.

Closing the loop

The camera is simple and no fuss, and I think the process of making them useful should feel the same. I want to close that loop.

I’ll be happy if this app can help keep at least one camera out of the landfill.
